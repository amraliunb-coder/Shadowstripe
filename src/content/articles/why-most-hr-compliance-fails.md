When small and medium enterprises (SMEs) attempt to replicate the HR infrastructure of massive corporations, the result is almost always a failure of execution. 

The problem isn't a lack of intent. Most founders want to do the right thing by their employees and the law. The problem is **practicality**.

## The Copy-Paste Policy Problem

We often see businesses downloading generic 100-page employee handbooks from the internet. They distribute them, nobody reads them, and they are promptly forgotten—until a crisis occurs. At that point, the business discovers that their "comprehensive" policy actually contradicts their real-world practices, leaving them exposed to significant legal risk.

### Three Steps to Practical Compliance:

1. **Audit your reality, not your aspirations.** Document what you actually do, not what you wish you did.
2. **Trim the fat.** If a policy doesn't apply to a 20-person company, don't include it just to look professional.
3. **Train for understanding, not just signature.** A signature on a handbook acknowledgment is a legal defense; actual understanding is risk prevention.
